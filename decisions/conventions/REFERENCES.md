# References for the conventions

The maintainer's list. "Checked" means compared with our formula, with the
equations named.

## Neutron scattering (CONV-0007)

- J. Jensen and A. R. Mackintosh, *Rare Earth Magnetism: Structures and
  Excitations* (Clarendon, Oxford, 1991), ch. 4; free chapter:
  https://www.fys.ku.dk/~jjensen/Book/echap4.pdf. Checked 2026-11-21: sec. 4.2
  (correlation function as matrix elements), (4.2.1)–(4.2.3c) (cross section,
  projector, ½gF(κ), e^{−2W} with W ≈ κ²⟨u²⟩/6, static and dynamic parts,
  χ″). Also (2.1.18) for CONV-0009.
- S. Raymond, "Magnetic excitations", Collection SFN 13, 02003 (2014),
  doi:10.1051/sfn/20141302003. Checked 2026-11-21: (1.4) cross section,
  (1.5) fluctuation–dissipation (no 1/π, unlike Jensen and Mackintosh
  (4.2.3c)). The source the old code cited as "the Enderle book following
  Stephane Raymond article".
- G. L. Squires, *Introduction to the Theory of Thermal Neutron Scattering*,
  3rd ed. (Cambridge University Press, 2012); chapter DOIs
  10.1017/CBO9781139107808.*. Not checked.
- S. W. Lovesey, *Theory of Neutron Scattering from Condensed Matter*, Vol. 2:
  Polarization Effects and Magnetic Scattering (Clarendon, Oxford, 1984). Not
  checked.
- A. T. Boothroyd, *Principles of Neutron Scattering from Condensed Matter*
  (Oxford University Press, 2020), doi:10.1093/oso/9780198862314.001.0001;
  ch. 6 magnetic cross section and form factors, ch. 8 excitations. Not
  checked.
- "Transition probabilities for f-electron J-multiplets in cubic crystal
  fields", J. Phys. Chem. Solids 33, 59–68 (1972),
  doi:10.1016/s0022-3697(72)80054-4 (attributed to Birgeneau, not confirmed).
  Not checked.
